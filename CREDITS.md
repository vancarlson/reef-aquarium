## Generated by this project

The seagrass blade, bubble and plankton textures, the sun-ray shaft, the backdrop gradient, the haze band, the soft
shadow and the caustic pattern are generated by the scripts in `tools/` or drawn from scratch. They are covered by this project's MIT licence.

## Software

Built with [Bevy](https://bevyengine.org/) (MIT or Apache-2.0) and the Rust ecosystem; asset preparation used OpenCV,
NumPy and Pillow. Licences of all Rust dependencies are in `THIRD_PARTY_LICENSES.md`.
